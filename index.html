04 / RESEARCH

Published research · 2026

OV
JOURNAL OF EMERGING PARADIGMS IN COMPUTING SYSTEMS · VOL. 3, ISSUE 02

OralVision: A Deep Learning Framework for Multi-Class Oral Disease Classification

Moiz Ud Din · Ibtisam Asghar · Ejlal Hameed · Saad Irfan Khan

This paper presents a deep learning framework for multi-class oral disease classification, exploring attention-enhanced feature extraction to support automated analysis of oral images. The study evaluates an EfficientNet-B0 architecture with Factorized Coordinate Attention using accuracy and macro-F1, and investigates generalization through external evaluation.

99.53%Internal test accuracy
0.9841Internal test macro-F1

Reported results are for the paper’s internal test evaluation; they are not external evaluation results or a claim of clinical diagnostic performance.
